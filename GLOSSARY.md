# Cadence

An online course platform where Students buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
Someone who takes courses on the platform.
_Avoid_: Learner, user (when the role is meant)

### Buying

**Purchase**:
One payment for a course, recording what was paid and where the buyer was.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**List Price**:
The price an instructor sets on a course, before any discount.
_Avoid_: Full price, base price

**PPP Price**:
A List Price lowered by a purchasing-power discount based on the buyer's country.
_Avoid_: Regional price, discounted price

**Team Seat**:
One place on a team's Purchase, which a single Student can claim to take the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence

**Seat Code**:
The code a Student enters to claim a Team Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon, coupon code

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, in their own words, to get their money back for a Purchase. Someone reviews it by hand and approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The amount paid on a Purchase, returned when a Refund Request is approved. After a Refund the buyer no longer owns the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period
